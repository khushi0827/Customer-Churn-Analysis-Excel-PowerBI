Raw Dataset → Data Cleaning in Excel → Data Preparation → Power BI Analysis → Interactive Dashboard → Business Insights

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### 1. Contract Type

Month-to-month customers show substantially higher churn compared with customers on one-year and two-year contracts.

### 2. Tenure

Customers with shorter tenure show a significantly higher churn rate, indicating that early-stage customers are an important retention segment.

### 3. Internet Service

Fiber optic customers show relatively higher churn compared with DSL and customers without internet service.

### 4. Payment Method

Electronic check users show the highest churn rate among the analyzed payment methods.

### 5. Charges

Higher charge categories are associated with higher customer churn.

### 6. Support Tickets

Customer churn was also analyzed in relation to technical and administrative support tickets to identify possible patterns in customer service interactions.
